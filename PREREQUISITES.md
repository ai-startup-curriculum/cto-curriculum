# Prerequisites for the Co-Founder / CTO track

This curriculum is a **role pathway** at level 25 in the Startup Leadership
family. It does not re-teach the material that lower-level tracks already own.
Come in with the assumed depth below, or work through the linked prerequisite
tracks first.

## Assumed lower-level tracks

The pathway assumes you are fluent with, or actively working through, the
following:

- **[`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations)**
  — shared prerequisite for the entire startup-track ecosystem. Company
  formation, US Delaware C-corp basics, stage vocabulary (`IDEA → PRE-SEED →
  SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE`), the FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA pillar map,
  and unit-economics fundamentals.
- **[`ai-infra-junior-engineer`](../ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning)** (level 10)
  — engineering-craft prerequisites: Linux, Git, Python (or one modern
  backend language), packaging, HTTP APIs, working command-line proficiency.
- **[`ai-infra-engineer`](../ai-infra-engineer-learning)** (level 20) — hands-on
  cloud infrastructure fundamentals on at least one major cloud provider
  (AWS or GCP): compute, storage, networking basics, IAM, one relational
  database, one queue, one object store, one CI/CD pipeline. The CTO still
  writes code some of the time and must be able to reason about the stack in
  detail with the team.

## Working knowledge you should bring

Independent of the tracks above, the reader should arrive with:

- One or more prior professional software-engineering roles (typically 5+
  years) — enough production experience to have shipped, operated, and
  supported software with real users.
- Comfort reading foundational engineering-leadership texts before or during
  the modules — Camille Fournier's *The Manager's Path*, Will Larson's
  *An Elegant Puzzle*, and Ben Horowitz's *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*
  are referenced in the module material and quoted at points.
- Willingness to be honest about which stage your startup is actually at.
  The material is stage-tagged; running the mod-106 30→150 material at
  month 3 of pre-seed will feel unmoored.

## Not taught here

- **The CEO's core job** — fundraising narrative construction, board
  management as CEO, hiring executives, capital allocation. Defers to
  [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum).
  The CTO learns *how to work with a CEO* here.
- **Deep product discovery, roadmap, and experimentation craft.** Defers to
  [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum).
  The CTO consumes enough to be an effective partner.
- **Deep HR / people-ops / compensation / performance-management craft.**
  Defers to
  [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum).
- **Deep FP&A / cap-table / SAFE / priced-round mechanics.** Defers to
  [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum).
- **Deep GTM / enterprise-sales-motion craft.** Defers to
  [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum).
- **Day-two engineering-management craft** (1:1s, feedback loops,
  performance-management, difficult conversations). Defers to
  [`ai-infra-team-lead`](../ai-infra-team-lead-learning) (level 30) — the CTO
  hires or promotes into that craft rather than living inside it.
- **Deep MLOps / ML platform / fine-tuning / training-pipeline engineering
  for AI-native startups.** Defers to `ai-infra-mlops` (level 25),
  `ai-infra-ml-platform` (level 30), `fine-tuning-engineer` (level 30), and
  `training-pipeline-engineer` (level 25). The CTO makes the build-vs-buy
  and platform-selection calls; those tracks own the depth.
- **Deep AI safety / red-team / risk engineering.** Defers to
  `ai-risk-engineer-learning` (level 25, AI Governance family) and
  `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40). The AI-native CTO adopts founder-
  scope hygiene from mod-107.
- **Deep platform / distributed-systems / multi-region architecture at
  post-Series-B scale.** Defers up to `ai-infra-senior-architect` (level 45),
  `ai-infra-principal-engineer` (level 50), and `ai-infra-principal-architect`
  (level 55).
- **Legal opinion.** The CTO briefs counsel; counsel delivers opinion.
- **The external audit opinion itself.** The CTO owns SOC 2 / ISO 27001
  readiness and evidence; the CPA firm delivers the attest opinion.

## Recommended reading before you start

The modules cite these throughout; a first pass beforehand pays back
compounding leverage:

- Camille Fournier — *The Manager's Path* (chapter on CTO)
- Will Larson — *An Elegant Puzzle* + *Staff Engineer*
- Ben Horowitz — *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*
- Elad Gil — *High Growth Handbook* (free online)
- Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais — *Team Topologies*
- Forsgren, Humble, Kim — *Accelerate* (and current DORA report at
  [dora.dev](https://dora.dev/))
- Charity Majors — *What is a CTO?* and *The Engineer/Manager Pendulum*
  essays
- Y Combinator Startup School Library — [`startupschool.org/library`](https://www.startupschool.org/library)
- Zach Goldberg — *The Startup CTO's Handbook* (open source)
