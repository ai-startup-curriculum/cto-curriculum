# mod-101 — The Co-Founder / CTO Role and the Ownership Map

> Where you sit on the CTO ladder, how the CTO seat relates to
> VP Eng / Chief Architect / Head of Platform, where this
> curriculum's coverage ends and each peer track begins, and
> the personal stage-by-stage plan for consuming it all in the
> right order.

**Planned time:** 10 hours (5 chapters + 4 exercises + 1 lab +
1 quiz)
**Track:** [`cto-curriculum`](../../README.md) — Co-Founder /
CTO, level 25
**Prerequisites:** [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations),
plus the engineering-craft prerequisites and recommended reading
in [`PREREQUISITES.md`](../../PREREQUISITES.md).

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish the four rungs of the CTO ladder — technical
  co-founder / founding engineer (pre-seed), hands-on CTO
  (seed, 3-10 engineers), player-coach CTO (Series-A, 10-25
  engineers), leadership-only CTO (Series-B+) — and locate
  yourself and your startup honestly.
- Distinguish the CTO role from the VP Engineering, Chief
  Architect, and Head of Platform roles — including when a
  startup should hire each as a separate person.
- Read the startup-track pathway coverage map and defend
  where *this* packet ends and where each peer track
  (`cpo-curriculum`, `founder-ceo-curriculum`,
  `startup-operations-governance-curriculum`,
  `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`,
  `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`) begins.
- Author a personal stage-by-stage development plan —
  which modules matter *now*, which will matter at the
  *next* transition, and which peer / higher-level tracks
  inherit the next chapter of the ladder.
- Read the four load-bearing texts (Charity Majors's *What
  is a CTO?* and *The Engineer/Manager Pendulum*, Ben
  Horowitz's *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*, Camille
  Fournier's *The Manager's Path* CTO chapter, Will
  Larson's *An Elegant Puzzle* + *Staff Engineer*) that
  are the shared vocabulary this curriculum builds on.

## Exercises

1. [CTO Ladder Self-Assessment](exercises/exercise-01-cto-ladder-self-assessment.md) — ~1 hour. Locate yourself on the ladder from calendar evidence, not from title. Feeds every other exercise in the module.
2. [CTO vs. VP Eng vs. Chief Architect Decision Drill](exercises/exercise-02-cto-vs-vp-eng-vs-chief-architect-decision-drill.md) — ~2 hours. Ten scenarios; explicit ownership assignment; the "which hat am I worst at" meta-question.
3. [Personal Stage-by-Stage Development Plan](exercises/exercise-03-personal-stage-by-stage-development-plan.md) — ~2 hours. Fills the four-quadrant template from chapter 04 into a two-to-three-page personal plan.
4. [Curriculum Scope and Deferral Contract](exercises/exercise-04-curriculum-scope-and-deferral-contract.md) — ~2 hours. One-page joint artifact with the CEO naming which pillars route where.
