## Load-bearing readings (chapter 05)

Read these before mod-102. Chapter 05 walks the *why* and the
suggested reading order.

- **Charity Majors** —
  - *"WTF is a CTO, anyway?"* — [charity.wtf/2019/09/16](https://charity.wtf/2019/09/16/wtf-is-a-cto-anyway/)
  - *"Engineering Management: The Pendulum Or The Ladder"* —
    [charity.wtf/2019/01/04](https://charity.wtf/2019/01/04/engineering-management-the-pendulum-or-the-ladder/)
- **Ben Horowitz** — *The Hard Thing About Hard Things* —
  [harpercollins.com](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-hard-thing-about-hard-things-ben-horowitz)
- **Camille Fournier** — *The Manager's Path* (O'Reilly) —
  [oreilly.com](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/) —
  the CTO chapter is the one chapter 02 of this module leans
  on hardest.
- **Will Larson** —
  - *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management* —
    [lethain.com/elegant-puzzle](https://lethain.com/elegant-puzzle/)
  - *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track* —
    [staffeng.com/book](https://staffeng.com/book)
