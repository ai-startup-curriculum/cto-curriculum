# Resources — mod-108-cto-ceo-and-board-communication

Primary references for the topics covered in this
module. Every entry is linked to a publisher, an
official standards body, a regulator, a legal
resource, or a canonical operator publication. See
[`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md)
for the full authoritative-reference list that
grounds the whole curriculum.

## The CTO↔CEO decision cadence (chapter 01)

### Founder-relationship framing

- **Y Combinator — Startup School Library** —
  [startupschool.org/library](https://www.startupschool.org/library) —
  the recurring lecture material on the co-founder
  relationship, cadence, and conflict-avoidance
  disciplines the chapter's decision-cadence framing
  sits inside.
- **Y Combinator — `The Startup CTO's Handbook`
  (Zach Goldberg)** —
  [github.com/ZachGoldberg/Startup-CTO-Handbook](https://github.com/ZachGoldberg/Startup-CTO-Handbook) —
  the open-source handbook that walks the CTO-seat
  discipline in the first eighteen months of a
  startup.
- **Ben Horowitz — *The Hard Thing About Hard
  Things*** —
  [harpercollins.com — The Hard Thing About Hard Things](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-hard-thing-about-hard-things-ben-horowitz) —
  the operator-canon reference on the founder-team
  dynamic under pressure.
- **First Round Review** —
  [review.firstround.com](https://review.firstround.com/) —
  recurring operator interviews on the CTO↔CEO
  relationship and decision cadence.

### Decision-rights framing

- **RACI / RAPID / DACI models** — recurring
  decision-rights frameworks in the operator
  literature. The chapter uses a bespoke four-column
  map rather than any single framework so it can be
  named against the four columns directly (CEO
  unilateral / CTO unilateral / consensus / board);
  the RACI family remains the vocabulary anchor for
  readers already fluent in it.

## The cofounder-dispute mechanic (chapter 02)

### Founder-scope legal templates and starting points

- **Y Combinator — Startup Documents** —
  [ycombinator.com/documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents) —
  the SAFE, the founder-agreement templates, and the
  post-money SAFE that anchor the counsel
  conversation.
- **Cooley GO — Startup documents** —
  [cooleygo.com/documents](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/) —
  Cooley's public library of startup templates:
  co-founder equity split, vesting schedules, IP
  assignment, and shareholders' agreements. The
  chapter cites these as *anchoring instruments*, not
  as a substitute for counsel.
- **Cooley GO — Confidential Information and
  Invention Assignment Agreement (CIIAA)** —
  [cooleygo.com — CIIAA](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/confidential-information-and-invention-assignment-agreement/) —
  the standard employee / contractor IP-assignment
  form that the chapter and the DD data room
  (chapter 04) both reference.
- **Founder Institute — Founder Collaboration
  Agreement** —
  [fi.co/insight/founder-agreements](https://fi.co/insight/founder-agreements) —
  the section outline for the cofounder-relationship
  agreement (the *non-legal* companion).

### Tax-timing and statutory references

- **IRS — About Form 15620 (Section 83(b)
  Election)** —
  [irs.gov — Form 15620](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-15620) —
  the standardised 83(b) election form and current
  IRS instructions. The 30-day filing window is
  irrecoverable if missed.
- **Delaware General Corporation Law §226 —
  Appointment of custodian or receiver for
  corporation in deadlock** —
  [delcode.delaware.gov — §226](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc07/index.html) —
  the statutory-deadlock remedy the chapter names
  as a last-resort backstop.
- **California Business and Professions Code
  §16600** —
  [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov — B&P §16600](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=7.&chapter=1.&part=2.&lawCode=BPC&article=) —
  the California statute that largely renders
  post-employment non-competes unenforceable, and
  the reason the cofounder-relationship agreement
  matters more in California than elsewhere.

### Founder-agreement operator reading

- **Noam Wasserman — *The Founder's Dilemmas*** —
  [press.princeton.edu — The Founder's Dilemmas](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691158303/the-founders-dilemmas) —
  the empirical study on founder-team disputes,
  vesting, and the mechanics of founder departure.

## Boundaries (chapter 07)

### Peer role-tracks the module explicitly cites

- **`founder-ceo-curriculum`** —
  [github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum) —
