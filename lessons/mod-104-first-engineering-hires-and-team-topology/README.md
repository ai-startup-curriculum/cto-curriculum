# mod-104 — First Engineering Hires and Team Topology

> How the pre-seed / seed / Series-A CTO turns a
> technical roadmap into a hiring plan a board can defend,
> an interview loop the org can run without the CTO in
> every debrief, and an org shape that survives eighteen
> months of scaling without a rewrite — instead of a
> series of one-off hires accumulated candidate by
> candidate.

**Planned time:** 20 hours (6 chapters + 6 exercises + 1
lab + 1 quiz)
**Track:** [`cto-curriculum`](../../README.md) — Co-Founder
/ CTO, level 25
**Prerequisites:** [`mod-101`](../mod-101-cto-role-and-ownership-map/README.md)
(especially chapter 05 on the shared reading vocabulary —
Fournier and Larson are the load-bearing books for this
module), [`mod-102`](../mod-102-architecture-under-uncertainty/README.md)
(the roadmap the hiring plan is authored *against*), and
[`mod-103`](../mod-103-build-vs-buy-and-platform-economics/README.md)
(every "build" disposition on the build-vs-buy matrix is a
hiring consequence this module absorbs).

## Learning objectives

- Author a **hiring plan against the technical roadmap** —
  role, seniority, timing, fully-loaded cost, funding-
  stage-appropriate compensation band — that survives a
  board conversation about runway and hiring pace.
- Design an **interview loop that scales beyond your first
  hires** — recruiter screen → technical screen → the
  take-home vs. pair-programming trade-off → values /
  bar-raiser interview → founder interview — and write the
  rubrics and calibration process the loop runs on.
- Make the first **founding-engineer hires** deliberately —
  the T-shaped-generalist / staff-archetype profile from
  Larson's *Staff Engineer*, the equity band, the founder-
  attachment questions, and the first-project design that
  turns the hire into a working colleague rather than a
  frustrated new starter.
- Apply the **Team Topologies** patterns
  (stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-
  subsystem) to the 5 → 15 → 25-engineer scale — a
  5-engineer startup is one stream-aligned team, a
  15-engineer startup is two stream-aligned teams plus an
  enabling role, and a 25-engineer startup is when a
  platform team starts to earn its keep.
- Decide when to hire the **first engineering manager**,
  the **first tech lead**, and the **first VP Engineering**
  — and reason about the **promote-vs-hire** trade-off
  honestly rather than reflexively.
- Produce an **org chart**, a **career-ladder v0**, and a
  **compensation band** that survives eighteen months of
  scaling without being rewritten from scratch.
- Cite the boundary to [`ai-infra-team-lead`](../../../ai-infra-team-lead-learning)
  (level 30) — that peer track owns **day-two management
  craft** (1:1s, feedback, performance management,
  difficult conversations); this module owns the CTO's
  **first-hiring and org-shape** decisions.
- Cite the boundary to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum)
  (People pillar) — that peer track owns **HR /
  people-ops / compensation depth**; the CTO consumes it
  and this module names where the hand-off is.

## Chapters

1. [The Hiring Plan Against the Roadmap and the Runway](01-hiring-plan-against-roadmap-and-runway.md) — role / seniority / timing / cost, the fully-loaded per-engineer number, and the board-defensible one-page plan.
2. [Designing an Interview Loop That Scales](02-interview-loop-and-rubrics.md) — the five-stage loop, the take-home vs. pair-programming trade-off, rubric authoring, and calibration.
3. [The Founding Engineer — Profile, Equity, First Project](03-founding-engineer-profile-and-first-project.md) — T-shaped generalist and Larson staff archetypes, equity band, founder-attachment interview questions, first-project design.
4. [Team Topologies at 5, 15, and 25 Engineers](04-team-topologies-at-startup-scale.md) — stream-aligned / platform / enabling / complicated-subsystem patterns applied to three concrete headcounts, and when the platform team starts to earn its keep.
5. [First EM, First Tech Lead, First VP Engineering — and the Promote-vs-Hire Trade-off](05-first-em-tech-lead-vp-eng-and-promote-vs-hire.md) — the honest triggers for each role, the two-column promote-vs-hire drill, and how to avoid the two default failure modes.
6. [Org Chart, Career-Ladder v0, and Compensation Band — Artifacts That Survive Eighteen Months](06-org-chart-career-ladder-and-comp-band.md) — the three artifacts the CTO owns, the peer-track hand-off to People / Governance, and the annual re-run rhythm.

## Exercises

1. [Hiring Plan Against Roadmap and Runway](exercises/exercise-01-hiring-plan-against-roadmap-and-runway.md) — ~3 hours. One-page hiring plan the CEO and the board can read; roles, seniority, months-of-runway consumption, comp band per role.
2. [Interview Loop and Rubrics Authoring](exercises/exercise-02-interview-loop-and-rubrics-authoring.md) — ~3.5 hours. Full loop for one open role including written rubrics, take-home vs. pair-programming decision, and the calibration protocol.
3. [Founding Engineer Profile and First Project](exercises/exercise-03-founding-engineer-profile-and-first-project.md) — ~2.5 hours. Written profile (archetype, must-haves, nice-to-haves, non-goals), founder-attachment interview kit, and a first-project brief.
4. [Team Topologies Mapping for Three Stages](exercises/exercise-04-team-topologies-mapping-for-three-stages.md) — ~2.5 hours. Three org-shape diagrams (5 / 15 / 25 engineers), each labelled with Team Topologies patterns and inter-team interaction modes.
5. [Promote-vs-Hire First-EM Drill](exercises/exercise-05-promote-vs-hire-first-em-drill.md) — ~2 hours. Two-column drill applied to a real (or realistic-composite) first-EM decision; a written decision memo the CEO can read.
6. [Org Chart, Career-Ladder v0, and Comp-Band Authoring](exercises/exercise-06-org-chart-career-ladder-v0-comp-band-authoring.md) — ~3.5 hours. The three artifacts, wired together, that a first-round investor's technical-DD reviewer could read in fifteen minutes.

## Lab

  (~3 hours) — planned. Bundles the outputs of exercises 01

  reviewer through. Scaffolded from the exercise outputs

## Quiz

- One quiz (~30 min) covering: the fully-loaded per-
  engineer cost model, the five stages of the interview
  loop, the take-home vs. pair-programming trade-off, the
  four Team Topologies team types plus the three interaction
  modes, the promote-vs-hire honest checklist, the three
  artifacts (org chart / career-ladder v0 / comp band),
  and the boundary to [`ai-infra-team-lead`](../../../ai-infra-team-lead-learning)
  and to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum).
