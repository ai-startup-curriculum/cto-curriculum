# Resources — mod-104-first-engineering-hires-and-team-topology

Primary references for the topics covered in this module.
Every entry is linked to a publisher, an author-maintained
URL, or an official standards body / vendor documentation
page. See [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md)
for the full authoritative-reference list that grounds the
whole curriculum.

## The shared reading vocabulary (used across every chapter)

These four books are the load-bearing references for the
whole module. They are introduced in [`mod-101` chapter
05](../mod-101-cto-role-and-ownership-map/README.md); this
module assumes readers already share the vocabulary and
cites the specific chapters most relevant to first-hiring
and org-shape decisions.

- **Camille Fournier** — *The Manager's Path* (O'Reilly) —
  [oreilly.com](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/) —
  the CTO and VP-Engineering chapters are the load-bearing
  reading for chapters 05 and 06 (leadership hires and the
  ladder v0).
- **Will Larson** — *Staff Engineer* (Stripe Press) —
  [staffeng.com/book](https://staffeng.com/book) — the
  Tech-Lead / Architect / Solver / Right-Hand archetype
  vocabulary used in chapters 03 (founding-engineer
  profile) and 06 (IC-track ladder anchors).
- **Will Larson** — *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of
  Engineering Management* —
  [lethain.com/elegant-puzzle](https://lethain.com/elegant-puzzle/) —
  the systems-of-engineering-management framing chapter
  05's triggers draw on.
- **Ben Horowitz** — *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*
  (HarperCollins) —
  [harpercollins.com](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-hard-thing-about-hard-things-ben-horowitz) —
  the executive-hiring and management-debt chapters are
  the reference for chapter 05 (VP Eng hire) and the
  exercise-05 memo.

## The hiring plan (chapter 01)

### Compensation-band references the plan cites

- **Levels.fyi** — [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/) —
  crowdsourced compensation by level and location; the
  seed / Series-A columns are thinner than the FAANG
  columns but the trajectory shape calibrates well.
- **Y Combinator — Startup School library** —
  [startupschool.org/library](https://www.startupschool.org/library) —
  YC's founder-facing guidance on founding-engineer
  compensation and equity ranges.
- **Y Combinator — Work at a Startup** —
  [workatastartup.com](https://www.workatastartup.com/) —
  YC's hiring surface for founder-stage roles; useful for
  calibrating on what the current YC-batch market pays and
  offers.
- **Carta — State of Startup Compensation** —
  [carta.com/blog](https://carta.com/blog/) — aggregate
  cap-table and equity data by stage; the CFO / People-lead
  reference for equity bands.
- **Index Ventures — Option Plan** —
  [indexventures.com/optionplan](https://www.indexventures.com/optionplan/) —
  Index's public tool for benchmarking option grants by
  role, seniority, and stage.
- **Pave** — [pave.com](https://www.pave.com/) — paid
  startup-compensation benchmark most People leads at seed
  / Series-A subscribe to; the CTO consumes the report.
- **Radford Global Technology Survey** —
  [radford.aon.com](https://radford.aon.com/) — the
  established enterprise-comp survey; more relevant at
  later stages than at seed.

### The peer-track boundary the plan defers to

- **`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`** (People
  pillar) —
  [github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) —
  the peer track that owns HR, people-ops, and
  compensation depth. The CTO consumes the People lead's
  benchmark reports rather than running the surveys.

  and the veto-by-construction discipline it operates on.

## The founding engineer (chapter 03)

### Equity-band references

- **Y Combinator — Startup School library** —
  [startupschool.org/library](https://www.startupschool.org/library) —

## The two explicit peer-track hand-offs

Every chapter in the module names one or both of these
boundaries; they are the specific interfaces the CTO
consumes rather than re-teaches.

- **`ai-infra-team-lead`** (level 30) —
  [`../../../ai-infra-team-lead-learning`](../../../ai-infra-team-lead-learning) —
  the peer track that owns **day-two management craft**:
  1:1s, feedback, performance-management, difficult
  conversations, coaching craft. This module owns the
  *decision* to hire the first EM (chapter 05) and the
  *ladder v0 that describes the EM role* (chapter 06);
  the day-two work of *being* that EM is the peer
  track's charter.
- **`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`** (People
  pillar) —
  [github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) —
  the peer track that owns **HR / people-ops /
  compensation depth**: employment law, benefits
  administration, payroll operations, running a
  performance-review cycle, compensation-band survey
  subscriptions and maintenance. This module owns the
  CTO's *first version* of the org chart, ladder v0, and
  comp band as engineering-org artifacts; the ongoing
  depth is the peer track's charter.
