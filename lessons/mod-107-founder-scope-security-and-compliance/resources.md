# Resources — mod-107-founder-scope-security-and-compliance

Primary references for the topics covered in this
module. Every entry is linked to a publisher, an

compliance page. See

## Founder-scope security posture (chapter 01)

### Enterprise-buyer questionnaires

- **Cloud Security Alliance — Consensus Assessments
  Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ)** —
  [cloudsecurityalliance.org — CAIQ v4](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/consensus-assessments-initiative-questionnaire-caiq-v4/) —
  the most widely-used vendor-security questionnaire
  format. The controls it screens on are the
  founder-scope Q1 (*"which controls, if absent,
  lose us deals?"*) shortlist.
- **Shared Assessments — SIG (Standardized
  Information Gathering)** —
  [sharedassessments.org — SIG](https://sharedassessments.org/sig/) —
  the other widely-used vendor questionnaire; some
  enterprise buyers require SIG rather than (or in
  addition to) CAIQ.

### Acquisition-DD framing

- **Andreessen Horowitz — technical due diligence
  checklist** —
  [a16z.com/tech-diligence-checklist](https://a16z.com/tech-diligence-checklist/) —
  the public a16z checklist chapter 01 references
  for the Q2 (*"which artifacts, if missing at
  Series-B DD, cost us a price cut?"*) framing.

### Coordinated-disclosure conventions the deferral list assumes

  `/.well-known/security.txt` convention.

### GDPR Data Protection Officer trigger

  [edpb.europa.eu — DPO guidelines](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-12017-data-protection-officers-dpos_en) —

## GDPR and CCPA (chapter 04)

### GDPR — the regulation and regulator guidance

- **General Data Protection Regulation** (Regulation
  (EU) 2016/679) —
  [eur-lex.europa.eu — GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) —
  the full text.
- **European Data Protection Board** —
  [edpb.europa.eu](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/) — the

  publishes the operative guidelines.
- **ICO (UK)** —
  [ico.org.uk — UK GDPR guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/) —

- **CNIL (France)** —
  [cnil.fr — Professionals](https://www.cnil.fr/en/home) —
  the French regulator; historically an active
  enforcer of cookie-consent rules.

## HIPAA at founder scale (chapter 05)

### The rules and HHS guidance

- **HHS — HIPAA for Professionals** —
  [hhs.gov — HIPAA](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/index.html) —
  the HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR) landing
  page; the authoritative reference for the
  Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification rules.
- **HHS — HIPAA Privacy Rule** —
  [hhs.gov — Privacy Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html).
- **HHS — HIPAA Security Rule** —
  [hhs.gov — Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html) —
  the technical safeguards at 45 CFR § 164.312 and
  the wider administrative / physical safeguards.
- **HHS — Breach Notification Rule** —
  [hhs.gov — Breach Notification](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html) —
  the 60-day OCR clock, individual-notification, and
  media-notification requirements.
- **HHS — Guidance on De-identification** —
  [hhs.gov — De-identification methods](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/special-topics/de-identification/index.html) —
  the 18 HIPAA identifiers (Safe Harbor) list.
- **HHS — Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (December
  2024) — HIPAA Security Rule modernisation** —
  [hhs.gov — HHS Announces NPRM on the HIPAA Security Rule to Strengthen Cybersecurity for Electronic Protected Health Information](https://www.hhs.gov/about/news/2024/12/27/hhs-announces-nprm-hipaa-security-rule-strengthen-cybersecurity-electronic-protected-health-information.html) —
  watch this rulemaking as it moves toward
  finalisation.
